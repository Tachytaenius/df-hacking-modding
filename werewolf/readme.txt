Safe (non-crazed) werewolves! :]
Intended for worlds with no werebeast curse types, but obviously you can do whatever you want.

The line:
	[MAXAGE:70:90] Should be same as human, which was tweaked from 60:120
is present in creature_werebeast.txt, but since people interested in having my werewolf mod raws probably don't have that change on their humans...
You can change that line to:
	[MAXAGE:60:120]
to make it consistent with vanilla DF humans.

Also I disabled the secret from being learnable (since I only generally put it on one custom guy in my worlds using DFHack). Search for () rounded brackets in interaction_secret_werebeast.txt and switch them to [] if you want it to be learnable.
