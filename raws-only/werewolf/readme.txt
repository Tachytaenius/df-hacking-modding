Safe (non-crazed) werewolves! :]
Intended for worlds with no werebeast curse types, but obviously you can do whatever you want.

Folders:
- "vanilla-raw" folder: werewolves for vanilla raws
- "tachy-raw" folder: werewolves for my raws. Matches a max age range change made for humans and also allows affecting my kemonomimi castes.

Also in both folders I disabled the secret from being learnable (since I only generally put it on one custom guy in my worlds using DFHack). Search for () rounded brackets in interaction_secret_werebeast.txt and switch them to [] if you want it to be learnable.
