interaction_syndromes

[OBJECT:INTERACTION]

From 50. Script is hermit-syndromes

Physical and mental attributes will rust as will skill, so the other syndromes are useful.
Also the skin wrinkles anyway. This mod's approach to fixing that bug (present as of 50.14) is to calculate
a value for how wrinkly the affected unit would be at their age and just move towards it every tick. Thus:
Requires DFHack
[INTERACTION:SYNDROME_WRAPPER_NO_AGING]
	[I_EFFECT:ADD_SYNDROME]
		[SYNDROME]
			[SYN_NAME:no aging]
			[CE_ADD_TAG:NO_AGING]

Eliminates skill rust
Requires DFHack
[INTERACTION:SYNDROME_WRAPPER_NO_SKILL_RUST]
	[I_EFFECT:ADD_SYNDROME]
		[SYNDROME]
			[SYN_NAME:no skill rust]
			[CE_SPEED_CHANGE:SPEED_PERC:100:START:0] Dummy effect (100% speed) to prevent the game from removing the syndrome

Ticks up mental attributes when below their (calculated) starting value
Requires DFHack
[INTERACTION:SYNDROME_WRAPPER_MENTAL_ATTRIBUTES_MINIMUM]
	[I_EFFECT:ADD_SYNDROME]
		[SYNDROME]
			[SYN_NAME:mental attributes minimum]
			[CE_SPEED_CHANGE:SPEED_PERC:100:START:0] Dummy effect

Ticks up physcial attributes when below their (calculated) starting value
Requires DFHack
[INTERACTION:SYNDROME_WRAPPER_PHYSICAL_ATTRIBUTES_MINIMUM]
	[I_EFFECT:ADD_SYNDROME]
		[SYNDROME]
			[SYN_NAME:physical attributes minimum]
			[CE_SPEED_CHANGE:SPEED_PERC:100:START:0] Dummy effect
